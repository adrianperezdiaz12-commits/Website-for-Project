About Nausha

Nausha formed in the backstreets of Toronto from a group of friends with common music taste, blending guitar and other instrument sounds with vibrant soundscapes. Nausha is an indie soft rock band that focuses on making reflective music that speak to one’s inner psyche.

Nausha’s second album, Feeling just plain terrible, captures the amazing sounds of passion and discovery, delivering tracks that move between energy and reflective music. This new album showcases how far the band has come since their humble beginnings, while staying true to the raw spirit that initially brought them together.
